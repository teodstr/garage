# Code of conduct

garage is built by volunteers: founders, students, mentors and anyone who wants to start something. Everyone should feel welcome here, whatever their level, background or language.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient.** For many contributors, this is their first pull request. Help them get it merged.
- **Criticize ideas, not people.** "This step is unclear" is useful feedback. A remark about the person who wrote it is not.
- **Assume good faith.** A short or clumsy message often comes from a language barrier, not from rudeness.
- **Give credit.** Cite your sources and thank the people whose work you build on.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination, in public or in private.
- Sexual remarks or images, and personal attacks.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or self-promotion unrelated to the project.

## Moderation

The maintainer can edit, hide or delete comments, close issues and pull requests, and block anyone who breaks these rules. A small problem gets a friendly reminder first. Serious or repeated problems lead to a ban.

## Reporting a problem

If someone makes you uncomfortable, tell the maintainer, [teodstr](https://github.com/teodstr). You can also use the "Report content" option that GitHub offers on every comment. Reports are handled discreetly.

These rules apply in every space of the project: issues, pull requests, reviews, and anywhere someone speaks on behalf of garage.
