# Roadmap

Skills, countries and languages we'd love to add. Each one is a good first contribution: pick one, open an issue saying you're on it, and follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Research

- [ ] `pricing-experiments`: test willingness to pay before launch (price tests on a landing page, Van Westendorp questions)
- [ ] `market-signals`: read demand signals from search trends, marketplaces, job posts and reviews

## Design

- [ ] `onboarding-design`: a first-use experience that gets new users to the core value fast
- [ ] `offer-packaging`: plans, tiers and bundles that match customer segments
- [ ] `product-roadmap`: from MVP to version 1, prioritized by evidence

## Resources

- [ ] `grant-application`: write a grant or competition application that answers the jury's criteria
- [ ] `crowdfunding-campaign`: plan a rewards-based campaign, from the page to the backers

## Launch and grow

- [ ] `cofounder-agreement`: the questions cofounders should settle early (roles, equity split, vesting, exit)
- [ ] `first-hire`: when and how to hire the first employee or freelancer
- [ ] `investor-update`: a short monthly update for investors and mentors
- [ ] `sales-discovery-call`: structure a B2B discovery call and a demo
- [ ] `content-plan`: content that brings the target customer to you
- [ ] `startup-metrics`: the few numbers to track at each stage
